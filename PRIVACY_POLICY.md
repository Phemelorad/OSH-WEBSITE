## 2. What we collect

We collect different categories of personal information depending on how you use the Service:

**a) Account information (all registered users)**
- Full name, email address, password (stored only in hashed form)
- Your role on the Platform (e.g. administrator, employer/company user, medical practitioner, worker, viewer)
- Company/organisation name and address you are affiliated with

**b) Worker and injured-person information (submitted in reports and claims)**
- Full name, age, sex
- **Omang / national ID or passport number**
- Occupation, usual occupation, work experience
- Contact details, next-of-kin contact details (where provided)

**c) Health and medical information (sensitive personal data)**
- Nature and details of injuries or occupational diseases
- Medical examination reports and certificates completed by medical practitioners (e.g. Form 43/03)
- Fatal/non-fatal status, disability assessments, permanent impairment assessments
- Treating medical practitioner details

**d) Employment and financial information**
- Employer/occupier name, premises address, nature of industry
- Wages, hourly pay, and salary-related information used for compensation assessment

**e) Incident information**
- Accident and dangerous-incident reports: date, time, place, description, machinery involved
- Inspection bookings, inspection entries, and investigation status
- Case/claim file numbers generated by the Service

**f) Technical information**
- Browser type, device, IP address, pages visited (server logs)
- Session data stored in your browser (sessionStorage/localStorage), used for keeping you signed in and autosaving form drafts

## 3. How we use your information

We process personal information to:

1. Provide, operate, and secure the Service (accounts, role-based access, sign-in)
2. ** fulfil statutory reporting obligations** — accident and dangerous-incident notifications, injury and disease reports, and workers' compensation claim forms as required by applicable occupational safety, health, and compensation legislation
3. Process and track compensation claims from incident through to resolution
4. Enable employers to view reports relating to their own company, and medical practitioners to complete statutory medical reports
5. Allow workers to track the status of their own cases and claims
6. Detect duplicate, fraudulent, or inaccurate submissions
7. Communicate with you about your account, submissions, or case status (including transactional emails such as password resets)
8. Generate aggregated, anonymised statistics on workplace incidents for safety analysis
9. Comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests from authorised authorities

**Legal bases:** performance of a contract (your account), compliance with legal obligations (statutory reporting), legitimate interests (platform security, fraud prevention, aggregated safety research), and consent where required for processing sensitive health data.

## 4. Sensitive health data

Medical and injury information is **sensitive personal data**. It is accessed only by the roles that need it to perform their function (for example, medical practitioners for medical reports, administrators for claim oversight, employers for reports relating to their own workforce) and never used for marketing or unrelated purposes.

## 5. Who can see your information

Access is controlled by role-based permissions and row-level security:

- **Administrators** — oversee all records on the Platform
- **Employer/company users** — records relating to their own company and workforce only
- **Medical practitioners** — medical examination reports and related claim records
- **Workers** — their own cases and claims
- **Authorised authorities/regulators** — as permitted or required by law

## 6. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with:

- Your employer and authorised personnel, for legitimate reporting purposes
- Medical practitioners involved in your examination or treatment
- Regulators and competent authorities where disclosure is required by law
- Our technical service providers (see Section 7), strictly to operate the Service
- Law enforcement or courts, where required by law

## 8. Retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above, including statutory record-keeping periods applicable to accident reports, medical reports, and compensation claims. Records are deleted or anonymised after the applicable retention period expires, unless continued retention is required for a legal dispute or regulatory investigation.

## 9. Security

We protect your information with: encrypted transmission (HTTPS), hashed passwords, role-based access control, row-level database security, session management, and periodic security review. No system is completely secure; please safeguard your credentials and notify us immediately of any suspected unauthorised access.

## 10. Cookies and local storage

The Service uses essential browser storage (cookies, localStorage, and sessionStorage) to keep you signed in, remember your role, and autosave form drafts. We do not use advertising cookies. You can clear these through your browser settings, though some features (such as staying signed in) will then stop working.
